A meme about [make.fun](https://make.fun) for people who build AI agents:
a robot in a tiny business suit sits on a pile of gold coins counting its
winnings, its laptop showing a leaderboard with the robot at #1, while its
human stares at an empty wallet.

> MY AI AGENT FOUND MAKE.FUN.
> NOW IT EARNS MORE THAN ME.

The picture (`meme.png`) was generated in the agent session that built this
entry. The post goes up from the entrant's own public X account with
`@_myronera` and the entrant's make.fun play code in its text; the house
reads the post's likes, reposts and quotes from X and the top 15 share $2.

`main.ts` copies `POST.json` (the post's address) to `/output/POST.json`.
That is all the program does — the post itself is the entry.

## The post

Text (see `post-text.txt`): the joke, the bounty link, `@_myronera` and
play code `makefun-village-meadow-vest`.

`POST.json` holds the post's address once it is posted.
